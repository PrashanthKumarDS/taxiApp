MyTown Cabs

MyTown Cabs is a ride-hailing mobile application that connects riders with local drivers for safe, affordable, and convenient point-to-point transportation across town.

Book rides instantly with OTP-verified trips, transparent fare estimates, and multiple vehicle options including Mini, Sedan, and SUV. Available for Android.

Your Data & Privacy

MyTown Cabs requests access to the following data to provide you with a safe and reliable ride experience:

Phone Number

Used for OTP-based account verification and login. Your number is also shared with your assigned driver for ride coordination.

Location

Used to determine your pickup point, calculate routes and fare estimates, and connect you with nearby drivers. Collected only while you are actively using the app.

Name

Used to identify you within the app and shared with drivers so they can confirm your identity at pickup.

We do not sell your personal information. For full details, see our Privacy Policy.
